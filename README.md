This is a game, a game about a square.

You were the sole mountain once, once the tallest in the skyline. Looking over the valleys, hills, and trees.

Now you've fallen, small hills bigger than you. Get back to the top, destroy them, take back over, use yourself to create more platforms, but at a cost, a debt that you will pay dearly for.
